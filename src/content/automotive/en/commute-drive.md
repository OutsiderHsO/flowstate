## Turn a dull route into a practice loop

Same road, same traffic. The only difference is whether you are willing to treat every launch as a rep.

### Three things worth practising deliberately

1. **The bite point**: the narrowest slice of clutch travel — find it with muscle, not with your eyes.
2. **Following distance**: hold a fixed gap instead of alternating between brake and throttle.
3. **Route memory**: learn the surface undulations so shifts happen where your eyes are looking, not where the wheels already are.

None of this depends on a particular car. It only depends on the driver.
