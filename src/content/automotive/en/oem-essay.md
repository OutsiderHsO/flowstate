## First: provenance

"Stock" describes a supply chain — part numbers, consistency, warranty.

## Second: condition

Here "stock" claims the car was **never modified**. It is a claim about time, not about parts.

## Third: a position

Some people saying "stock" are making an aesthetic argument: I trust the factory's balancing act over my own single-point optimisation.

## Closing

One word, three meanings. Plenty of arguments never resolve simply because the two sides are not talking about the same thing.

> A shared word, and still two different worlds.
