## A gathering with no organiser

The quiet miracle of a parking-lot meet is that it needs **almost no organising**. Information moves by word of mouth, a few group-chat photos and the phrase "the usual spot".

> The rules are invisible, but the cost of breaking them is immediately visible.

## Field notes

- Arrivals cluster inside a ninety-minute window;
- Cars settle into rings based on who already knows whom;
- The late-night exit is tidier than the entrance.

All of the above is illustrative only and describes no real event.
